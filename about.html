Our story

Built from a straight-answer problem.

GROWEXA TECHNOLOGIES started as a two-person shop running ads for local businesses that couldn't get a straight answer from bigger agencies. Nine years later we're a full studio of strategists, designers, engineers and media buyers.

We don't hand you off to a junior account manager after the pitch. The people who plan your strategy are the people who build it, run it, and report back on what actually happened to your numbers.

Today that studio covers paid media, web and application development, SEO and AI automation — four disciplines most clients used to buy from four different vendors who never spoke to each other.
