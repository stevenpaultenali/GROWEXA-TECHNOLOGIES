FAQ

Questions we hear before the first call.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope — a landing page and a full application aren't priced the same. Every quote is fixed and given in writing before any work begins, after a short discovery call.

Do you work with businesses outside our size or industry?

We work with small businesses through mid-size companies across most industries. If you're unsure whether we're a fit, the audit call is free and we'll tell you honestly.

Will I own the website and ad accounts?

Yes. Every account, domain and codebase is set up under your ownership from day one — nothing is held hostage if you ever choose to leave.

How long does a typical project take?

A marketing website usually takes 3–5 weeks; ad campaigns can launch within a week of approval. Custom applications vary by scope, and timelines are set during the strategy stage.

Do you offer ongoing support after launch?

Most clients move into a monthly retainer for optimization, reporting and updates. It's optional, and we'll say so upfront if your project doesn't need one.
